# talent-share content structure

## Main promise

Find a program and learn with someone you can ask. Live interaction is the central value. Program information helps a seeker choose; the app tools support the learning experience.

## Homepage sequence

1. Recognise the offer: a marketplace for live online and in-person programs, with an immediate Explore programs action.
2. Recognise the problem: getting stuck with recorded content, uncertainty about program fit, and wanting to know the provider before paying.
3. Understand the solution: learn live, ask about the part you do not understand, and practise with a provider. Introduce providers and seekers in context.
4. Find personal relevance: subject guides help visitors identify an interest and what to consider. Current programs remain in the app.
5. Reduce uncertainty: explain profiles, reviewed applications, available reviews, levels, schedules, requirements, and checkout totals. Do not imply every program has reviews.
6. Understand the experience: connect schedule, resources, discussion, and video to practical needs after booking.
7. Make the next step clear: explain finding, checking, booking, and attending. Repeat the app CTA after visitors understand the commitment.
8. Offer a provider path: explain discovery and organisation benefits, then link to the dedicated page.
9. Resolve questions: live format, account requirements, price, selected dates, and cancellation conditions.
10. Give a final action: explore programs, with a separate provider application link.

## Supporting page roles

- `/teach/`: provider problem, marketplace discovery, profile credibility, program creation, management tools, application steps, questions, and application CTA. No guaranteed enrollments or earnings claims.
- `/learning-experience/`: before, during, and after a session. Communication tools are demonstrated where questions and updates arise.
- `/subjects/` and six guides: help seekers choose a subject and prepare questions before browsing current programs. These are editorial content, not mirrored app inventory.
- `/how-it-works/`: explain the steps and reduce uncertainty about choosing and booking.
- `/trust/`: explain provider information, reviews, payments, enrollment access, and cancellation conditions.
- `/faq/`: answer remaining practical questions with searchable static content.
- `/about/`: explain the marketplace and learning/teaching model.

## Conversion intent

The primary next action is exploring programs in the app. The secondary action is beginning a provider application. The website does not add an unrelated email gate or duplicate app signup and booking flows.

A future measurement plan can compare app click-through, program views, enrollment completion, and provider application completion. These require appropriate analytics and app integration. They are not implemented here, and this revision makes no claim of a measured conversion increase.

## Voice and evidence

Natural, direct language. No long-dash sentence punctuation. Brand is `talent-share`; roles are `providers` and `seekers`; offerings are `programs`. Established formats such as workshops or consultations can still describe how a program is delivered.

The existing website's stated problems inform the narrative. The supplied feature brief supports product claims. Stock photography and example interface messages are labelled as illustrations. No customer proof or program inventory is fabricated.
